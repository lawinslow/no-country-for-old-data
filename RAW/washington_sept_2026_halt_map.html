Where buses stand still on Washington St

Every stationary event on the corridor — two or more consecutive position fixes at essentially the same place — placed by where it happened and when. Weekdays only, 3 days, pre-school-year. Filled dots are halts more than 35 m from any stop, sized by how long the bus stood; hollow dots are dwell at a stop. Dashed verticals are the stops. Fixes more than 40 m off the Washington St centreline are dropped, which removes the Belgrade Ave routes and the Forest Hills busway.

Grey blocks are the bus lanes — each covers a stretch of street and a window of hours. Inbound, 5–10am: Roslindale Sq north to Forest Hills since 2018, plus Albano→Poplar striped in 2023. Outbound, 2–7pm: Forest Hills south to Firth Rd/South St since 2021. The gap between Poplar and the Square is left unshaded — the 2023 extension is documented as ending at Poplar and the 2018 lane as starting at the Square, and the City's text does not settle whether that gap is real.
