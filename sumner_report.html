Sumner Elementary School — MCAS Analysis

Originally prepared March 2026; updated October 2026 with SY2026 results. Data: DESE / E2C Hub, SY2017–2026. Boston Public Schools. All scores are avg scaled score, Next Gen MCAS scale.

Summary: Sumner Elementary closed after SY2024–25 and merged with Philbrick Elementary into the Sarah Roberts School, which reported MCAS results for the first time in spring 2026. Its 327 tested students roughly match Sumner and Philbrick combined the year before (324), so it is essentially the same population under one roof.
  • Overall, the first merged year is flat. Sarah Roberts averaged 487 in ELA and 487 in math. The combined Sumner+Philbrick averages for 2025 were 488 and 488. Both are close to the BPS average (486 / 487) and well below the state (493 / 495).
  • Grades 3–4 are strong; grades 5–6 are weak. Grade 3 matched last year's predecessor-school grade 3, and grade 4 was 6–9 points above last year's grade 4. Both are above BPS. Grades 5–6 are the weak spot: grade 5 scored 481 in ELA and 480 in math, 6–7 points below BPS, and grade 6 ELA (478) is the school's lowest score.
  • Following the same students, the year was slightly negative. Cohorts that moved up a grade into Sarah Roberts mostly scored 3–7 points lower than they did the year before at Sumner or Philbrick. Grade 6 math was the only exception (+2). Some decline from grade to grade is typical in BPS, but this is a softer first year than the flat school-wide average suggests.
  • Philbrick's strong 2025 didn't carry over. Philbrick (about 60 tested students) had its best year ever in 2025, at 500 ELA and 505 math. In a school five times its size that advantage is diluted, and the merged school looks much more like Sumner than Philbrick.
  • Sumner's own history is unchanged: a deep COVID drop in math, a partial recovery, and ELA that never returned to 2019 levels before the school closed.

3. Cohort Analysis

Each line follows one group of students across grades, labeled by the year they were in Grade 3. For example, the 2022 line is Gr3 in 2022, Gr4 in 2023, and so on. Dashed lines are pre-COVID cohorts. Open markers are 2026 scores at Sarah Roberts, where each cohort now sits alongside former Philbrick students.

Cohort observations:
  • ELA cohorts mostly decline from grade to grade after COVID. The 2022 cohort went 492 → 491 → 487 → 482 (Gr3 → Gr6). The 2023 cohort went 489 → 487 → 482 → 478, with the last point at Sarah Roberts.
  • Math cohorts are mixed. The 2021 cohort entered at the low point (473) and climbed to 488 by grade 6, the clearest within-school gain in the data. The 2022 cohort held between 484 and 488. The 2023 cohort went 482 → 489 → 479, then rebounded to 484 in grade 6 at Sarah Roberts.
  • The 2024 cohort kept slipping after the merger. It went 488 → 482 → 481 in ELA and 482 → 481 → 480 in math (Gr3 → Gr5), which explains the weak 2026 grade 5. The 2025 cohort held steady from Gr3 to Gr4 (494 → 493 ELA, 490 → 491 math) and remains the school's strongest.
  • Year-to-year changes at a school this size (60–90 students per grade) move by several points from noise alone. Treat single-step changes under about 5 points as noise.

Caveats: One year of results for a new school can't separate the effect of the merger from ordinary year-to-year noise. These are school-level aggregates, so the data can't show which students came from which building, and the 2026 enrollment may include students from outside either predecessor school. Merger years often bring disruption from a new building, new staff and combined classrooms, so a flat first year isn't a verdict on the school.

5. Key Findings

  • Sarah Roberts' first year matched its predecessors' combined average. ELA was 487 versus 488, and math was 487 versus 488. Meeting or Exceeding is 33% in ELA and 32% in math, in line with BPS (29% / 29%).
  • The lower grades are the bright spot. Grades 3–4 scored 491–496 in both subjects, at or above BPS.
  • Grades 5–6 are the weak spot. Grade 5 (481 ELA, 480 math) is 6–7 points below BPS grade 5, and grade 6 ELA (478) is the lowest score in the building. Both are older cohorts that were already slipping at Sumner, and they slipped another 3–7 points in the first merged year.
  • Sumner's math recovery was incomplete when it closed, and its ELA never got back to 2019 levels. Grade 4 ELA fell from 498 in 2019 to 482 in 2025.
  • Philbrick's gains were real but small in scale. Its 2023–2025 math surge (505 school-wide in 2025) came from about 60 students. That's not enough to move a school of about 330, and the 2026 results don't show it spreading.
